# YouTube Upload Checklist

## Before upload
- [ ] Watch the complete exported file with sound.
- [ ] Confirm the title, description, thumbnail and actual video match.
- [ ] Confirm the five answers and explanations.
- [ ] Check spelling, captions, sound levels and runtime.
- [ ] Confirm music and visual assets are licensed for the intended use.
- [ ] Prepare a short, accurate description and any source credits that are required by licenses.

## Audience and policy
- [ ] Assess whether the video is directed to children and set the audience designation accurately in YouTube Studio. PuppyToons' preschool quiz videos will generally be child-directed.
- [ ] Review current YouTube child-safety, made-for-kids, synthetic/altered content and monetization guidance as applicable.
- [ ] Do not add misleading claims, manipulative engagement prompts or requests for children's personal information.
- [ ] Do not assume that AI-generated content is automatically original, factual, compliant or eligible for monetization.

## Suggested metadata pattern
**Title:** Azula's Animal Adventure Quiz 🐶 | 5 Fun Animal Questions for Kids

**Description:**  
Join Azula, the PuppyToons Golden Retriever puppy, for five friendly animal questions! Look at the choices, think before the answer reveal, and learn a little animal fact each time. Made for young learners and families.

**Thumbnail:** Azula on one side, two or three large friendly animal illustrations, and a short readable phrase such as “5 ANIMAL QUESTIONS”. Avoid showing the correct answer to every question on the thumbnail.

## After upload
- [ ] Watch the published version once to verify processing and playback.
- [ ] Check captions and thumbnail crop on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Record episode topic, upload date, runtime, asset folder and any corrections needed for the next episode.
- [ ] Use viewer analytics to learn what is understandable and useful; do not treat any single metric as a guarantee of future performance.
