# Child-Friendly Quality Checklist

## Learning accuracy
- [ ] Exactly five unique questions.
- [ ] Each question has one unambiguous correct answer.
- [ ] All options are suitable for the target age.
- [ ] Every explanation is accurate and supported by a reliable source.
- [ ] No answer is revealed early in narration, subtitles, visual emphasis or sound.

## Child experience
- [ ] Wording is clear, warm and age-appropriate.
- [ ] Every question includes enough time to think (usually 8–12 seconds).
- [ ] The video invites participation without shame or pressure.
- [ ] No request for a child's full name, location, contact details or other personal information.
- [ ] No frightening imagery, unsafe challenges, violence, stereotypes or distressing sounds.
- [ ] Visuals are calm enough to follow; no strobe effects or frantic flashing.

## Continuity and visuals
- [ ] Azula matches the approved reference: fur, eyes, ears, proportions and teal-blue collar.
- [ ] No duplicate puppy, extra limbs, morphing or unexplained new objects.
- [ ] Option images and labels match.
- [ ] Text is legible and spelled correctly.
- [ ] All answer highlights occur only at the reveal.
- [ ] Transitions are clean; no abrupt frame or audio jumps.

## Audio and rights
- [ ] Narration is understandable and consistent.
- [ ] Music never masks speech.
- [ ] Every sound effect and music track has a usable license or is original.
- [ ] No unauthorized voice cloning or use of a real child's likeness.

## Final export
- [ ] Entire video watched from start to finish.
- [ ] Runtime is 6–8 minutes unless the episode brief says otherwise.
- [ ] Export plays and has correct aspect ratio.
- [ ] Captions/subtitles checked manually.
- [ ] Title, thumbnail and description accurately describe the video.
- [ ] YouTube audience designation and applicable disclosure/policy settings checked.
