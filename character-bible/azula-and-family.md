# PuppyToons Character Bible

## Azula — lead host

- **Species/breed:** Golden Retriever puppy.
- **Age impression:** tiny young puppy; exact age need not be stated.
- **Appearance:** fluffy golden-brown coat, floppy ears, expressive warm brown eyes, small black nose, rounded puppy proportions and a teal-blue collar.
- **Personality:** curious, affectionate, gently mischievous, eager to help, delighted by discovery. She can be silly without frightening or humiliating anyone.
- **Movement:** small bouncy steps, tail wag, head tilt, soft blink, gentle paw lift. Avoid frantic movement during the question and thinking pause.
- **Voice:** optional short puppy reactions; keep them brief and consistent. The main quiz narration should remain clear and easy to understand.
- **Never change:** fur color, face, ear shape, eye color, body scale, collar color or proportions. Exactly one Azula in a shot unless a deliberate mirror is part of the story (avoid this by default).

## Family cast (optional)

Use the family in story episodes when relevant; keep quiz episodes focused on Azula unless the story genuinely benefits from a family member.

- **Mom Sarah:** brown hair in a bun, beige sweater, dark jeans; warm and patient.
- **Dad David:** brown hair, short beard, brown plaid shirt, khaki pants; warm and playful. A blue baby carrier may appear only when specified for that scene.
- **Maya:** 4-year-old girl, brown hair, colorful striped shirt, purple cape, dark pants and yellow boots.
- **Leo:** 9-month-old baby boy, short light-brown hair, blue/green striped baby outfit.

## Style guide

- Polished, family-friendly 3D animation with appealing rounded forms, soft realistic fur, expressive faces, cinematic but gentle lighting and warm color design.
- Widescreen 16:9 by default.
- Keep backgrounds simple enough that children can focus on the question.
- Use bright contrast and uncluttered layouts; avoid flashing effects, strobe-like transitions and overwhelming visual noise.
- No copyrighted characters, recognizable branded costumes or copied logos.

## Continuity block

Include this paragraph in prompts whenever Azula is visible:

“Azula matches the approved PuppyToons character reference exactly: one tiny fluffy golden-brown Golden Retriever puppy, floppy ears, expressive brown eyes, small black nose, puppy proportions and the same teal-blue collar. Preserve her face, fur, markings, scale and collar. Exactly one Azula; no duplication, morphing, extra limbs, wardrobe changes or teleporting.”

## Voice anchor template

Use one stable voice description and repeat it exactly across the episode:
“PuppyToons narrator: friendly adult female voice, warm and clear, medium-slow pace, natural General American English, bright but not shrill, smiling delivery, careful pronunciation, consistent tone and volume in every scene.”

The anchor is a prompt aid, not a guarantee of perfect voice matching. Listen to the finished audio and fix noticeable drift.
