## 1. Episode story

Azula discovers an explorer bag in a sunny garden. Inside is an empty animal-sticker board. She needs help solving five friendly animal mysteries to complete the board. Each answer earns a sticker. The final board reveals a cheerful animal collage.

## 5. Narration script

### Intro — about 25 seconds
“Hi, little explorers! Azula is ready for an animal adventure. Can you help her solve five animal mysteries? Look at the choices, think carefully, and say your answer out loud. We’ll wait so you have time to guess. Every time we solve a mystery, Azula gets an animal sticker. Ready? Let’s go!”

### Question 1 — Elephant
“Uh-oh! Azula found a giant footprint beside the garden pond. Which animal has a long trunk? Is it A, an elephant; B, a rabbit; C, a penguin; or D, a turtle? Look carefully at the choices. Which one has a long trunk? Say your answer out loud. Take your time!”
**[Pause 8–12 seconds. Show the same options; do not highlight any answer.]**
“The answer is A, the elephant! An elephant uses its trunk to smell, breathe, drink and pick things up. Great thinking! Azula has earned an elephant sticker.”
**[Soft reveal chime; Azula places one elephant sticker on the board.]**

### Question 2 — Kangaroo
“Azula has found a trail of funny hop marks. Which animal is famous for hopping and carrying a joey? Is it A, a kangaroo; B, a goldfish; C, an owl; or D, a snail? Look at each choice. Which animal hops along the ground? Tell Azula your guess!”
**[Pause 8–12 seconds; no answer cue.]**
“It’s A, the kangaroo! A baby kangaroo is called a joey, and kangaroos move by hopping. You solved another mystery! Azula adds a kangaroo sticker.”
**[Azula does one tiny hop in place; gentle sparkle.]**

### Question 3 — Cow
“Shhh! Azula hears a sound from the farm. Listen carefully!”
**[Play a gentle, licensed or original cow lowing sound.]**
“Which farm animal usually says ‘moo’? Is it A, a cow; B, a duck; C, a cat; or D, a horse? Think about the sound you heard. Which animal do you think made it?”
**[Pause 8–12 seconds; no answer cue.]**
“It’s A, the cow! A cow makes a sound called a low, and people often write that sound as ‘moo.’ Let’s listen one more time.”
**[Play the same sound softly.]**
“Nice listening, explorers! Azula earns a cow sticker.”

### Question 4 — Turtle
“Azula is exploring a tiny garden path. She sees four animal friends. Which animal carries a shell on its back? Is it A, a turtle; B, a dog; C, a frog; or D, a sheep? Look closely at the pictures. Which one has a shell?”
**[Pause 8–12 seconds; no answer cue.]**
“The answer is A, the turtle! A turtle’s shell is part of its body and helps protect it. Well done! Azula watches her turtle friend take a slow, gentle walk and adds a turtle sticker.”
**[Do not show a turtle leaving or removing its shell.]**

### Question 5 — Zebra
“Azula has one last mystery. She sees some animal patterns. Which animal is known for black-and-white stripes? Is it A, a zebra; B, a lion; C, a bear; or D, a flamingo? Look at the choices. Can you find the animal with stripes? Say your guess!”
**[Pause 8–12 seconds; no answer cue.]**
“It’s A, the zebra! Zebras have distinctive black-and-white striped coats. You solved all five animal mysteries! Azula places the final sticker on her board.”
**[The board fills with five simple animal stickers; warm celebratory music.]**

### Recap — about 55 seconds
“You did it, little explorers! Let’s remember our five animal friends. The elephant has a long trunk. The kangaroo hops, and its baby is called a joey. The cow makes a sound people write as ‘moo.’ The turtle has a protective shell. And the zebra has black-and-white stripes. Can you remember all five? Elephant, kangaroo, cow, turtle, zebra! Great thinking. Azula is proud of your curiosity!”

### Outro — about 25 seconds
“Thanks for learning with Azula on PuppyToons! Keep being curious, kind and ready to discover something new. Give yourself a quiet little cheer for trying your best. We’ll see you on the next adventure. Bye-bye, little explorers!”

### Intro shots
1. **IN-01 (6 sec):** Wide garden establishing shot. Azula sits beside a colorful explorer bag and an empty sticker board. Leaves move gently. Locked medium-wide camera.
2. **IN-02 (6 sec):** Azula sniffs the closed bag, then looks up with curious brown eyes. One action, medium shot.
3. **IN-03 (6 sec):** Azula gently opens the bag with one paw; show five blank sticker spaces on the board. Keep props fixed and distinct.
4. **IN-04 (7 sec):** Azula sits beside the board, tail wagging slowly, looking toward the viewer as the narrator explains the game.

### Reusable question pattern (repeat for Q1–Q5)
For each question, create these shots:
- **Qx-01 Setup (8 sec):** Azula discovers the episode's clue prop in a simple, friendly environment. One action.
- **Qx-02 Question (7 sec):** Clean question card with four large illustrated answer cards. No answer highlighted.
- **Qx-03 Options (8 sec):** Hold the same clean card while narrator reads choices. Keep labels and images stable.
- **Qx-04 Thinking pause (10 sec):** Static card; subtle floating dots or a gentle pulsing border around the whole card, not any individual answer. No narration after the initial “Take your time.”
- **Qx-05 Reveal (6 sec):** Only after the pause, highlight the correct answer. Keep other options visible but unhighlighted.
- **Qx-06 Explanation (10 sec):** Show the correct animal in a simple, friendly context while narrator explains the fact.
- **Qx-07 Payoff (7 sec):** Azula adds one sticker to the board and reacts happily. One sticker only.

### Per-question scene details
- **Q1 Elephant:** Clue = large footprint by pond. Options: elephant, rabbit, penguin, turtle. Reveal elephant only after pause. Payoff: elephant sticker.
- **Q2 Kangaroo:** Clue = hop marks on garden path. Options: kangaroo, goldfish, owl, snail. Reveal kangaroo only after pause. Payoff: one tiny hop in place, then sticker.
- **Q3 Cow:** Clue = gentle farm sound. Options: cow, duck, cat, horse. Use a licensed/original cow sound before the thinking pause; do not make the sound itself a giveaway if it reveals the answer too early. Better: play it after the question, then pause; repeat after reveal.
- **Q4 Turtle:** Clue = garden path and four animal cards. Options: turtle, dog, frog, sheep. Reveal turtle only after pause. Show shell attached to the turtle's body.
- **Q5 Zebra:** Clue = four animal pattern cards. Options: zebra, lion, bear, flamingo. Reveal zebra only after pause. Payoff: final sticker completes the board.

### Recap and outro shots
1. **RECAP-01 (10 sec):** The five stickers are visible in separate spaces; narrator begins recap.
2. **RECAP-02 (15 sec):** Show one friendly animal illustration at a time in the same order as narration: elephant, kangaroo, cow, turtle, zebra.
3. **RECAP-03 (10 sec):** Wide shot of Azula sitting proudly beside the completed sticker board.
4. **OUT-01 (10 sec):** Azula waves one paw gently toward the viewer; leave clean space for “Thanks for learning with PuppyToons!”
5. **OUT-02 (10 sec):** Azula walks a few small steps toward the garden gate, turns back and wags her tail. End on a stable medium-wide frame.

**Important:** The reusable shot pattern produces more than one clip per question. Adjust clip durations and add gentle holds in editing to hit the 6:45 target. Do not rush narration or remove the thinking pauses merely to fit the target.
