# Episode 001 — Azula's Animal Adventure Quiz

**Category:** Animals and pets  
**Audience:** Ages 3–8  
**Language:** Simple English  
**Format:** 16:9 landscape  
**Runtime target:** 6:30–7:00  
**Status:** Draft example — fact-check and review the final visuals before publishing.

## Story premise

Azula finds a colorful explorer bag in the garden. To earn five animal stickers for her learning board, she asks the viewer to help solve five animal mysteries. Each answer earns a sticker; the episode ends with a cheerful recap.

## Intro narration (about 20–25 seconds)

“Hi, little explorers! Azula is ready for an animal adventure. Can you help her solve five animal mysteries? Look at the choices, think carefully, and say your answer out loud. We’ll wait so you have time to guess. Ready? Let’s go!”

## Question 1 — The long trunk
- **Question:** Which animal has a long trunk?
- **Options:** A) Elephant; B) Rabbit; C) Penguin; D) Turtle.
- **Correct answer:** Elephant.
- **Explanation:** An elephant uses its trunk to smell, breathe, drink and pick things up.
- **Azula setup:** Azula discovers a large footprint beside a watering hole and sees four friendly animal picture cards.
- **Thinking cue:** “Look at all four animals. Which one has a long trunk? Take your time!”
- **Reveal:** Highlight Elephant only after the pause.
- **Payoff:** Azula gives a happy tail wag and earns an elephant sticker.
- **Fact-check note:** Verify the explanation with a reputable zoo or natural-history source before publishing.

## Question 2 — The hopping friend
- **Question:** Which animal is famous for hopping and carrying a joey?
- **Options:** A) Kangaroo; B) Goldfish; C) Owl; D) Snail.
- **Correct answer:** Kangaroo.
- **Explanation:** A baby kangaroo is called a joey; kangaroos move by hopping.
- **Azula setup:** A trail of gentle hop marks leads to four animal cards.
- **Thinking cue:** “Which animal hops along the ground? Can you spot it?”
- **Reveal:** Highlight Kangaroo.
- **Payoff:** Azula does one tiny, safe hop in place and earns a kangaroo sticker.
- **Fact-check note:** Confirm wording for the target age with a reputable wildlife or zoo source.

## Question 3 — The animal that says “moo”
- **Question:** Which farm animal usually says “moo”?
- **Options:** A) Cow; B) Duck; C) Cat; D) Horse.
- **Correct answer:** Cow.
- **Explanation:** A cow makes a lowing sound that people often write as “moo.”
- **Azula setup:** Azula hears a gentle farmyard sound and tries to guess which animal made it.
- **Thinking cue:** Play a short, gentle cow sound, then pause. “Which animal do you think it is?”
- **Reveal:** Show the cow and play the sound again at a comfortable volume.
- **Payoff:** Azula tilts her head, then proudly places the sticker on her board.
- **Fact-check note:** Use a licensed or original sound effect.

## Question 4 — The home in a shell
- **Question:** Which animal carries a shell on its back?
- **Options:** A) Turtle; B) Dog; C) Frog; D) Sheep.
- **Correct answer:** Turtle.
- **Explanation:** A turtle's shell is part of its body and helps protect it.
- **Azula setup:** Azula finds a tiny garden path with four animal illustrations.
- **Thinking cue:** “Look carefully. Which animal has a shell?”
- **Reveal:** Highlight Turtle.
- **Payoff:** Azula watches a friendly cartoon turtle walk slowly along the path.
- **Fact-check note:** Ensure the art does not imply a turtle can leave its shell.

## Question 5 — The animal with black-and-white stripes
- **Question:** Which animal is known for black-and-white stripes?
- **Options:** A) Zebra; B) Lion; C) Bear; D) Flamingo.
- **Correct answer:** Zebra.
- **Explanation:** Zebras have distinctive black-and-white striped coats.
- **Azula setup:** Azula sees four pattern cards and needs help matching the striped animal.
- **Thinking cue:** “Can you find the animal with stripes? Say your guess!”
- **Reveal:** Highlight Zebra.
- **Payoff:** Azula earns the final sticker; the five stickers form a little animal picture.
- **Fact-check note:** Use a reputable zoo or natural-history source for the final fact check.

## Recap narration (about 40–50 seconds)

“You did it, little explorers! We found the elephant with a trunk, the hopping kangaroo, the moo-saying cow, the turtle with its shell, and the striped zebra. Can you remember all five? Great thinking! Azula is proud of you.”

## Outro narration (about 15–20 seconds)

“Thanks for learning with Azula on PuppyToons! Keep being curious, kind and ready to discover something new. Give yourself a quiet little cheer, and we’ll see you on the next adventure. Bye-bye!”

## Visual and editing notes

- Use one consistent 3D animated style and the approved Azula reference.
- Keep all option cards large, with a distinct animal image and short label.
- During thinking time, do not highlight, zoom toward or otherwise hint at the correct option.
- Use a calm 8–12 second pause for each question.
- Keep the sound effects soft and the narration clearly audible.
- The script is a starting draft. Time it aloud, add meaningful transitions and verify facts and licensing before publishing.
